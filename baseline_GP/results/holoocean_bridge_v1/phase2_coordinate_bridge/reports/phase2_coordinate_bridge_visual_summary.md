## Reading Rule

- Grid cell `(row, col)` maps to HoloOcean `[x, y, z]`.
- `x = col * 5.0` meters.
- `y = -row * 5.0` meters.
- Increasing `col` moves right in both views.
- Increasing `row` moves down in the grid and toward more negative `y` in HoloOcean.
